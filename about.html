Our story

Fixing the CRM trust gap

Our founder, Allen Trinidad, spent years as a HubSpot subject-matter expert supporting HubSpot Partner agencies, then went independent. The same three sentences came up on almost every project.

From founders: "we bought HubSpot and we are still working in spreadsheets." From sales leaders: "I do not trust the forecast." From marketing: "we cannot tell you which campaigns produced revenue."

None of those are HubSpot problems. They are what happens when a CRM gets configured quickly, never architected, and never documented. The platform is capable. It was just never set up to match how the business actually runs.

PipelineSync was founded in 2025 to fix that specific problem. Not an agency selling campaigns, but a technical consultancy focused on CRM architecture, data quality, automation, and reporting. Five years of hands-on HubSpot work, and more than 30 businesses later, the pattern is still the same one we started with.

"We work directly with you, not through layers, to build a CRM your team trusts and uses every day."

Allen Trinidad, Founder

Our team

Eight people, one platform

PipelineSync is a team of eight: our founder, five HubSpot implementation specialists, and two supporting social media and administrative staff. Everyone who touches client work is on our team. We do not subcontract builds.

We are based in the Philippines and work US business hours. That structure is deliberate. It gives clients senior HubSpot capability at a rate that a US or UK agency with the same depth cannot match, and it means work moves on your calendar, not ours.

There is a second reason we built the team this way. The Philippines has a large pool of skilled remote professionals whose work is undervalued because the roles available to them are undervalued. We train ours into genuine HubSpot specialists, with certifications, real project experience, and career progression. Our clients get people who are invested in the platform rather than passing through it.

How we work

Three things we do differently

One platform, all the way down

HubSpot architecture is the whole business. We are not adding CRM work to a service list that also includes advertising and brand design. When the problem is a broken data model or a routing rule dropping leads, depth is what solves it.

Fast, and you can reach us

A dedicated implementation team means work moves in days rather than sprint cycles. You talk to the people building your system. We reply within 24 hours on business days.

Everything documented

Every build ships with SOPs, data model documentation, automation logic, and a permission matrix. Your team can run, change, and explain the system without us.
