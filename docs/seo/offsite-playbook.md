## 2. Google Business Profile (GBP)

### 2.1 Eligibility decision: `TODO(owner)`

Google's rules (verify against the current "Guidelines for representing your business on Google"):

- A GBP listing is only for businesses that make **in-person contact with customers during stated hours**. Online-only or remote-only businesses are not eligible.
- A **service-area business** (you travel to customers) hides its address and lists service areas. A **storefront** shows an address customers can visit, staffed during stated hours.
- Virtual offices, mailboxes, co-working addresses you do not staff, and "borrowed" addresses are violations and a common cause of suspension.

What this means for PipelineSync:

| Option | When it is allowed | SEO value | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Listing at the Philippines office | Only if the office is a real, staffed location where clients or visitors can meet the team during the posted hours (and signage/verification is possible). | Helps branded searches and the knowledge panel. Little value for "HubSpot consultant" searches in the US/AU/CA/UK, since GBP ranks by proximity. | Hours: posted hours must be local Philippines time. US business hours become overnight local hours, which can look odd and must match reality. Pairing a Philippines address with a US phone number is inconsistent: consider the WhatsApp/PH number as the GBP primary phone. |
| B. Service-area listing | Only if staff actually visit customers in the listed areas. | Low for this business model. | High if no in-person visits happen. |
| C. No GBP | Remote-only delivery, no staffed client-facing office. | None from GBP; rely on HubSpot directory, Clutch, LinkedIn. | None. |
| D. US address | Only a real, staffed US location. Do **not** use a virtual office. | Medium. | Suspension if not genuine. |

## 6. Review workflow

### 6.1 Rules

- Ask **every** client at the same point in the project. Do not pre-screen for happy clients (review gating is against Google's policy and the FTC rule on fake reviews and testimonials in the US).
- No incentives, discounts or gifts for reviews. No reviews written by staff, friends or family. Never write a review for a client to "approve".
- One platform per ask. Order: HubSpot Solutions Directory (clients on HubSpot) → Clutch → Google (only if GBP exists) → Upwork (Upwork clients only, through Upwork's own flow).
- Never add `Review` or `AggregateRating` schema for PipelineSync on the site.
- Quote a review on the site only with the client's permission, word for word, with the platform named.

### 6.2 When to ask

1. At handover, after training and SOPs are delivered: tell the client a short review request will follow.
2. 7–14 days after go-live (or after the first month of a retainer): send the request.
3. Retainers: once per year.

### 6.3 HubSpot setup

1. Create a deal property "Review request status" (Not sent / Sent / Reviewed / Declined) and "Review platform".
2. Create a sequence "Client review request" (Sales Hub or Service Hub seat with sequences required: **verify** your tier).
3. Enrol the main client contact manually from the deal when the "Handover" stage is reached, or create a task for the account owner from a deal workflow.
4. Unenrol on reply. Set "Reviewed" when the review appears; reply to it on the platform.
5. Report monthly: requests sent, reviews received, platform.

### 6.4 Email template (sequence)

**Step 1, day 0. Subject:** `Quick favour: a review of the {{ deal.dealname }} project?`


**Step 3, day 10:** task for the account owner to mention it on the next call. No further emails.

## 8. Don'ts

- No paid links, link exchanges, or "guest posts" on sites that sell placements.
- No private blog networks (PBNs), link farms, or mass directory submission tools.
- No fake, incentivised, staff-written or purchased reviews. No review gating.
- No virtual-office or borrowed addresses on GBP, Bing or Apple.
- No keyword-stuffed business names (e.g. "PipelineSync HubSpot Consultant US"). Name is "PipelineSync".
- No doorway pages per country or city.
- No client names, logos or results in outreach or profiles without written permission.
- No AI-spun copies of site content on third-party sites. Every guest piece is original.
