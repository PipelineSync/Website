Focus: HubSpot services for B2B buyers, US first, then Australia, Canada, UK (expressed through copy and `areaServed`, not per-country pages). Keyword priorities are qualitative; confirm volumes and difficulty in Google Search Console (after 4–6 weeks of data), Ahrefs, or Semrush before committing content budget.

## Rules

- One primary keyword per URL. `/solutions/` targets the hub term **"HubSpot consulting services"** so the proposed sub-pages (`/solutions/hubspot-implementation/`, `/solutions/hubspot-migration/`, …) can own their specific terms without cannibalising it. Until those pages exist, `/solutions/` keeps the anchors for each service.
- Titles: primary keyword first, ≤ 60 characters before the `| PipelineSync` suffix where possible (Google truncates around 580 px). Descriptions: 140–160 characters, specific benefit, no keyword stuffing.
- H1 keeps the brand voice; the keyword goes in the H1 or the first sentence under it, never in hidden text.

## Open content issues found during the audit

- Home lists "Sales Hub Enterprise" in the certification strip; `/about/` lists "Sales Hub Software". Confirm the correct certification name.
- Home pain cards show "30%+ duplicate rate" and "~8 hrs/rep lost" as symptoms with no source. Keep them clearly framed as typical symptoms or add a source.
- The automation article slug says "10 hours" while the title says "hours every week". Keep the URL (it is indexed); either source the 10-hour figure in the copy or keep the softer wording.
- The three template pages (governance checklist, migration mapping sheet, lead routing decision tree) are labelled Notion / Google Sheet / Miro but do not link to an actual file. Publish the files (and link them) before promoting the templates; until then they are thin pages.
- Resource pages are 350–450 words. The refresh schedule in `content-plan.md` §6 expands them.
